# RC.1 hardware documentation snapshots

These are public technical extracts of the hardware documents recorded in the 9 November 2026 OpenAMRobot 2.0 design baseline. They describe design intent and pending engineering decisions. They provide no fabrication, wiring, commissioning, or safety acceptance.

| Snapshot | Baseline and source revision |
|---|---|
| [Detailed hardware BOM (XLSX)](OpenAMRobot-2.0-Hardware-BOM-issue-7.16-public.xlsx) | Issue 7.16, 28 November 2026. Replaces Issue 7.15. Changes are listed in [`issue-7.16-changes.md`](issue-7.16-changes.md). |
| [Battery Guide (DOCX)](../../electrical/power_distribution/B-02-Battery-Guide-rev18.11-public.docx) and [PDF](../../electrical/power_distribution/B-02-Battery-Guide-rev18.11-public.pdf) | B-02 rev18.11, 8 November 2026. Drive revision `0B8N2fWq0nh5rZytzVUQ4T0x0NWtjYVcwbmpURHU5MGk1WUJVPQ`. The PDF was converted from this public DOCX. |

The workbook keeps the 149 component records from the detailed Hardware BOM sheet, with component identifiers, quantities and scope. The public workbook omits commercial and owner columns, internal planning worksheets, comments, and ancillary purchasing notes. The battery guide omits procurement allowances, internal Drive links, and an embedded image whose redistribution provenance was unverified. Third-party product identifiers and links identify references. The extracts do not bundle vendor documents.
