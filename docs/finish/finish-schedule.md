Reading the columns:

- **Material and current finish:** from the MMP drawing (material, sheet thickness and note 8), the
  mechanical BOM or the hardware BOM row named in the part column. "verify" means no source states it.
- **Proposed colour:** RAL reference and the sRGB screen value used by the model. "as delivered" means
  the part is not finished and the model keeps its own materials.
- **Status:** `decided` when the cited sources establish material, colour and process; `verify` names
  the open check.
